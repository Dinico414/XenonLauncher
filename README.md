# Xenon Launcher

A modern, minimalist, and highly customizable Android experience built from the ground up with Jetpack Compose.

---

## 🚀 What Makes Xenon Special?

Xenon Launcher isn't just another home screen—it's designed for users who want total control over their aesthetics without the complexity of traditional launchers.

### 🎨 Visual Icon Management
Forget searching for resource names in text fields. Xenon features a **full visual grid browser** for icon packs. Browse, search, and preview every single icon in a pack visually before applying it.

### 🌍 Global & Local Theming
Switch your entire device's look in seconds with **Global Icon Packs**. Want more precision? Use **Manual Overrides** to customize specific apps—Xenon smartly preserves your individual choices even when you swap global themes.

### 🎵 Immersive Media Experience
A dedicated **Media Page** that transforms based on what you're listening to. Featuring dynamic background blurring and dominant color extraction, your launcher UI adapts to your album art in real-time for a truly immersive feel.

### 📅 Intelligent "At a Glance"
Stay on top of your day with a sophisticated **Calendar Integration**. It goes beyond simple listing; it monitors your account sync status, handles multi-day boundaries, and provides a unified view of all your accounts seamlessly.

---

## ✨ Core Features

- **Icon Customization**: Support for adaptive shapes (Circle, Squircle, Teardrop, etc.), custom zoom levels, and icon shadows.
- **Frosted Aesthetics**: Integrated frosted glass (blur) effects and "Blacked Out" mode for pure AMOLED dark themes.
- **Advanced Search**: Unified search engine for Apps, Contacts, Files, and Web results.
- **Efficiency Dock**: Pinned apps and a versatile Floating Action Button (FAB) with customizable double-tap and long-press triggers.
- **Cloud Backup**: Securely save your settings and custom icon layouts to the cloud for easy migration between devices.
- **Privacy**: Hide sensitive applications from both the app drawer and search results.

---

## 🛠 Technical Stack

- **UI Framework**: 100% Jetpack Compose for a declarative, fluid interface.
- **Design System**: Material 3 (M3) with full support for dynamic color.
- **State Management**: Robust MVVM architecture utilizing StateFlow and Coroutines.
- **Image Loading**: Coil for high-performance, asynchronous image and icon processing.
- **Data Persistence**: Shared Preferences with JSON-based overrides for complex configurations.
